The mental model

Darktrace watches traffic and SaaS activity, learns each device's pattern of life, and raises a model breach when behaviour departs from it. AI Analyst then stitches breaches into incidents, and RESPOND (formerly Antigena) can act on its own.

Probe / vSensor
Collects traffic: a physical probe on a SPAN or TAP, or a virtual sensor in a cloud or on a host.
Master
The appliance or cloud instance that analyses everything and runs the Threat Visualizer UI.
Pattern of life
The learned baseline per device, per user, per subnet, and for the whole network.
Device
Anything with an IP or identity Darktrace tracks. Has a type, tags, and a credential history.
Model
Detection logic: conditions plus thresholds, grouped by category. Can be edited or cloned.
Model breach
One firing of a model for a device, with a breach score.
AI Analyst incident
Related breaches investigated and summarised into incident events.
RESPOND / Antigena
Autonomous response: block connections, enforce the pattern of life, quarantine a device.
Rarity
How unusual a destination is for the whole network: "rare" means few devices have ever visited it.
Tags
Labels on devices (Admin, Server, Antigena All) that models and response key off.

Reading a breach

Model name anatomy

CategoryUsually means
CompromiseStrong signs of infection: beaconing, C2, known-bad
Anomalous ConnectionUnusual destinations, ports or volumes
Anomalous FileUnusual downloads or uploads (EXEs, scripts, archives)
Anomalous Server ActivityA server behaving out of character
DeviceA device's own behaviour changed: new activity, scanning
Unusual ActivityLower-confidence deviations
UserCredential and account behaviour
CompliancePolicy issues: cleartext passwords, remote access tools, Tor
Antigena / RESPONDAutonomous response fired

Scores

Breach score
How strongly this one breach deviates, as a percentage. High doesn't always mean malicious.
Model priority
How important the model's author thinks it is (0–5).
Incident score
AI Analyst's confidence in an incident. Triage by this first.
Device score
How much a device is currently breaching. A rising score means investigate the device, not one breach.

Triage workflow

StepAsk
IncidentWhat does AI Analyst say happened? Which devices, which period?

Advanced Search

Lucene-style queries over the parsed protocol metadata. @type picks the protocol log, and @fields.* the values.

text@type:conn AND @fields.source_ip:10.10.4.22 AND @fields.dest_port:445
text@type:dns AND @fields.query:*.example-cdn.top
text@type:http AND @fields.host:files.example.com AND @fields.method:POST
text@type:ssl AND @fields.server_name:*duckdns.org
text@type:smb AND @fields.source_ip:10.10.4.22 AND @fields.action:write
@typeHas
connEvery connection: IPs, ports, protocol, bytes, duration
dnsQueries and answers
httpHost, URI, method, user agent, status
sslSNI (server_name), certificate, JA3
smb, dcerpcFile shares and remote procedure calls
kerberos, ntlmAuthentication, with usernames

Tune: field names vary by version. Open a result and click a field to filter on it rather than typing names from memory.

Autonomous response

ActionEffect
Block matching connectionsStops this specific behaviour (destination and port)
Enforce pattern of lifeAllows only what the device normally does
Quarantine deviceCuts all traffic to and from the device
Block connections to destinationStops every device reaching a domain or IP
Human confirmation modeActions wait for an analyst to approve

Read: check whether RESPOND is in autonomous or human-confirmation mode for the device. Many "it should have blocked this" cases are that setting.

Hunting in SecOps

Darktrace model breaches by device

udmmetadata.vendor_name = "Darktrace" nocase
$host = principal.hostname
match:
  $host
outcome:
  $breaches = count(metadata.id)
  $models = array_distinct(security_result.rule_name)
order:
  $breaches desc

Tune: check where your Darktrace feed puts the model name (rule_name or summary) in the UDM viewer first.
