Visual reference for Mimecast email security on cases: where it sits in mail flow, what each protection layer does, how URL rewriting works, how to trace a message and its clicks, and how to pull a bad message back out. Addresses, domains and IDs below are placeholders.

The mental model

MX records point at Mimecast, so every inbound message passes its gateway policies before it reaches Microsoft 365 or Exchange. Targeted Threat Protection (TTP) keeps protecting after delivery: links are rewritten and checked on click, and attachments are sandboxed.

Secure Email Gateway
Mimecast as your MX: inbound and outbound mail passes its policies.
URL Protect (TTP)
Rewrites links in mail and checks the destination each time someone clicks.
Attachment Protect (TTP)
Sandboxes attachments, or delivers a safe converted copy while the original is checked.
Impersonation Protect (TTP)
Scores mail for lookalike domains, display-name spoofing and other BEC signs.
Internal Email Protect
Scans internal and outbound mail, to catch a compromised account sending phish.
Held / hold for review
Messages a policy stopped. Admins (and sometimes users) release or reject them.
Message tracking
Search one message's route: received, processed, held, rejected, delivered.
Threat Remediation
Removes a delivered message from every mailbox it reached, and can restore it.

A rewritten link

Read: the rewritten link holds no full destination, only the domain. To see the real URL and who clicked it, open the TTP URL logs.

Investigation flow

StepAsk

Other recipientsSearch the same sender, subject or attachment hash across the whole organisation

Impersonation logsWhich BEC signs fired: new domain, similar domain, display name?

Watch for: in the audit log, new permitted senders or bypass policies, and admins releasing held messages that were held for malware or impersonation.

Containment

GoalHow
Pull a delivered phishThreat Remediation: remove by message ID, sender or attachment hash
Stop more arrivingBlocked senders policy for the address or domain
Stop clicks workingAdd the URL or domain to the managed URL block list
Contain a compromised userReset password, revoke sessions (Entra), check their outbound mail in Internal Email Protect

Hunting in SecOps

Mimecast deliveries from a sender

udmmetadata.vendor_name = "Mimecast" nocase
network.email.from = /@lookalike-domain\.example$/ nocase

Clicks allowed on rewritten links

udmmetadata.vendor_name = "Mimecast" nocase
target.url != ""
security_result.action = "ALLOW"
$user = principal.user.email_addresses
match:
  $user
outcome:
  $clicks = count(metadata.id)
  $urls = array_distinct(target.url)

Tune: Mimecast log types map to different UDM event types (email vs URL clicks). Check one event of each in the UDM viewer first.
