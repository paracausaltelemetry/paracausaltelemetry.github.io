Pwn2Play 2025

B.P.O

Miscellaneous Medium Author: T.A.R.S  ·  Pwn2Play 2025

Scenario

A rusted field case is discovered at a decommissioned signals facility near Bletchley Park. Inside: wartime documents, six damaged forensic artefacts, and a reel of encrypted radio traffic dated February 1944. The cipher system used is unclear from documentation alone.

Objective

Recover all required cipher machine settings from the six clues, then use them to decrypt the provided ciphertext. The machine in question is a Typex, the British military's rotor cipher machine, the closest Allied equivalent to the German Enigma.

The six artefacts

  • Audio recording · rotor clue
  • Video file · ring setting clue
  • Image · initial value clue
  • Network capture (.pcap) · plugboard clue
  • File metadata · rotor clue
  • Encoded data · final rotor / IV clue

Five artefacts yield rotor settings (Rotor · Ring Setting · Initial Value). One yields the plugboard configuration. All six are required to reconstruct the full Typex key and decrypt the intercept.

Machine type

Typex
British WWII rotor cipher machine. 5 rotors, reflector, plugboard. Operationally similar to Enigma but considered more secure.

The Numbers Mason

Cryptography Easy Author: T.A.R.S  ·  Pwn2Play 2025

Scenario

A short-wave intercept lands on a Cold-War listening post, addressed to callsign RED OCTOBER. The transmission is clipped, in Russian, and ends with a repeated string of decimal numbers, the kind of numbers-station traffic that runs to a recipient who already has the pad in hand. The package was intercepted in Sector 4, extraction is incomplete, and Archangel Depot is warning that local assets are compromised. The numbers are the key.

Objective

Pull the repeated decimal byte sequence out of the transmission, treat it as a one-time-pad key, base64-decode the accompanying ciphertext, and XOR the two together to recover the flag. The OTP guarantee of perfect secrecy holds only when the key is used once. Repetition is exactly what makes this transmission solvable.

Provided artefacts

  • message.txt · Russian-language intercept carrying the base64 ciphertext and the repeated numeric pad
  • transmission.wav · audio carrier of the same intercept · ~2 MB

Both artefacts encode the same payload through different channels. Either can drive the solve; together they reinforce that the numeric sequence is the intended key, not flavour text.

Flag format

P2P{…}

Cipher type

One-time pad (XOR)
Provably unbreakable when the key is truly random, secret, and used exactly once. Reusing the pad collapses it into a stream cipher and hands the plaintext to anyone listening.

Skills tested

Audio analysis Base64 XOR / OTP Decimal encoding Cold-War cryptanalysis
