After the exercise

Check your own footprint

You just rebuilt a stranger's life from what he left public. Now turn it on yourself. Work through these on your own accounts and fix what you can tonight.

Keep your results private. The only thing you share with the room is one number: how many settings you changed.

01 Fitness and location apps

A privacy zone hides your doorstep but draws a ring around it. Hiding the start and end of every activity is what actually works.

02 Photos and metadata

One photo with GPS left on undoes a careful privacy zone. Even with location off, the content of a photo still talks.

03 What is public about you

You are the best placed person to find out what you leak. Look at yourself the way an analyst would.

04 Usernames and accounts

A reused handle ties your running, gaming, work and forum lives to one person. That is the single easiest pivot to shut down.

05 Passwords and breaches

A password from an old breach may still open your accounts today, and a date of birth plus a home town is most of what you need to impersonate someone.

06 Unsolicited contact

The approach in the exercise starts with a friendly message. Treat out-of-the-blue offers and connections as suspect until proven otherwise.
