Where you've seen this before

This is the honeytrap pattern from the briefing: Hamas's fake profiles against IDF soldiers, APT36 against the Indian military, Iran recruiting through fake job offers. A believable persona, a move to a private channel, flattery, then a request that serves the attacker.

A fuller look at the approach

Jamie is not the only one. A wider sweep turns up more of the same operation. These are observations, not conclusions. Record what you can see, then take the techniques to the attribution portal and decide for yourself who this resembles, and how sure you can be.
