Rules of engagement

  1. 01Self-contained

    Everything you need is in these pages. You never need to touch a real website, person or map.

  2. 02An area, not an address

    Good OSINT names a town and a confidence, not someone’s front door.

  3. 03Show your working

    Write down where each answer came from. A claim without a source is a guess.

  4. 04Time box

    Rough guide: forty-five minutes on Part 1, then regroup as a room for Part 2.
