Operation Heatmap

You have been handed the public online traces of one person, a serving soldier who has been careless. Work the sources, answer the field questions, then work out how exposed he is and how you would protect him.

Rules of engagement

  • Everything here is self-contained. Every answer is in these pages. You never need to touch a real website, person or map.
  • Report an area, not an address. Good OSINT names a town and a confidence, not someone’s front door.
  • Pivot on what repeats. One reused handle ties most of these accounts together. Find it early.
  • Rough time box: 45 minutes on Part 1, then regroup for Part 2.
